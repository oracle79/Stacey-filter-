02 / The email growth lab

Make the list work harder.

The goal isn't to force email attribution to 25%. It's to create incremental, profitable revenue while protecting deliverability and customer trust.

The big unlock

From campaigns
to customer timing.

Filterbaby has a naturally recurring product. The highest-value emails may be the ones triggered by what a customer bought, when they bought it, and what they do next.

FLOW AUDIT

Audit before rebuilding

Inventory each flow, trigger, exclusion, offer, conversion and revenue attribution window. Look for gaps and overlap.

Checklist

Welcome, browse abandon, cart abandon, checkout abandon, post-purchase, installation, replenishment, subscription upcoming order, failed payment, cancellation, winback, review request, referral, and cross-sell.

SEGMENTATION

Send by customer state

Stop treating all subscribers as one audience. Use purchase and subscription behavior where data and consent permit.

Useful segments

Shower-only, faucet-only, multi-product, active subscriber, skipped refill, cancellation intent, recent purchaser, high-repeat customer, and engaged email recipient.

EXPERIMENTS

Measure incremental lift

Attributed revenue is not the same as revenue caused by email. Keep a holdout when feasible.

Scorecard

Incremental contribution per eligible customer, order rate, unsubscribe and spam complaint rates, discount cost, deliverability, and repeat behavior. Run tests long enough to capture the buying cycle.

04 / AI for the team

Give every function an AI co-pilot.

AI creates leverage when it is attached to a recurring workflow, approved data and a human owner—not when everyone is randomly prompting chatbots.

Creative strategy desk

Turn customer voice into a weekly creative brief.

  • Cluster reviews, comments and support tickets by objection.
  • Generate 20 hooks and 5 testable concepts per theme.
  • Tag results and feed winners back into the next brief.

Email campaign assistant

Speed up production while keeping strategy human-led.

  • Draft variants for different lifecycle segments.
  • Check links, claims, tone and segment exclusions.
  • Summarize tests with a “what changed / what next” report.

Customer intelligence

Make qualitative feedback searchable.

  • Summarize tickets and reviews weekly.
  • Find root causes behind returns, churn and confusion.
  • Draft a ranked backlog for product and CX.

Executive operating brief

Reduce time spent assembling updates.

  • Summarize weekly KPI movements and anomalies.
  • List decisions required, owners and due dates.
  • Keep forecasts and actual results clearly separated.

Product & research analyst

Accelerate discovery without outsourcing judgment.

  • Compare competitors' public offers and claims.
  • Turn interview transcripts into themes and questions.
  • Flag unsupported claims for legal/scientific review.

Internal knowledge base

Make existing company knowledge reusable.

  • Approved SOPs, product specs, claim substantiation and FAQs.
  • Searchable answers for new hires and support.
  • Version control and clear source citations.
WEEKLY CADENCE

Monday: insight → brief

AI summarizes the prior week's creative, CX and retention signals. The owner chooses 3–5 experiments, not 50.

CONTROL

Keep humans in the loop

Never auto-publish scientific claims, customer-facing promises, legal statements or sensitive data outputs without review.

SUCCESS METRIC

Measure time saved + quality

Track hours per asset, turnaround time, test velocity, error rate and business outcomes—not prompts generated.

05 / Execution

90 days to a clearer growth engine.

Sequence the work so measurement comes before scaling, and quick wins don't distract from retention economics.

DAYS 1–30 · DIAGNOSE

Find the leaks

  • Audit all email/SMS flows and deliverability.
  • Build new-customer CAC and contribution views.
  • Map subscription cohorts, skips and cancellation reasons.
  • Tag top ad creatives and customer objections.
  • Inventory team AI workflows and data permissions.
Deliverable: ranked opportunity backlog
DAYS 31–60 · TEST

Prove the winners

  • Launch highest-priority lifecycle tests.
  • Test shower-to-faucet cross-sell timing.
  • Run structured creative concept batches.
  • Trial one creator workflow and one AI workflow.
  • Use holdouts or controlled tests when feasible.
Deliverable: lift + margin evidence
DAYS 61–90 · SCALE

Systemize what works

  • Scale only profitable, repeatable tests.
  • Document SOPs and dashboard definitions.
  • Expand winning creator and lifecycle programs.
  • Assign owners, targets and review cadence.
  • Build the next-quarter investment case.
Deliverable: operating growth plan
06 / The exit lens

Build a business buyers can underwrite.

A 15× EBITDA outcome is an ambition, not a valuation assumption to build a plan around. Focus on the traits that could justify a premium.

What makes the business more valuable?

Growth quality matters.

A buyer will care about durable cash generation, evidence behind the growth, customer concentration, product/IP defensibility, channel risks and how dependent the company is on its founders.
